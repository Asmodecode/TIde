Keyboard shortcuts
==================

On a Mac, :kbd:`Ctrl` is :kbd:`Cmd`.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 70

   * - Keys
     - Does
   * - :kbd:`F5`
     - ▶ Run: build, send and start.
   * - :kbd:`Ctrl+E`
     - Build. :kbd:`Ctrl+Shift+E` picks a new place for the ``.8xp`` or ``.tns``.
   * - :kbd:`Ctrl+S`
     - Save.
   * - :kbd:`Shift+Alt+F`
     - Format document.
   * - :kbd:`Ctrl+B`
     - Show or hide the side bar.
   * - :kbd:`Ctrl+Shift+O`
     - Open folder; in a file, its functions, labels or ``Lbl``\ s.
   * - :kbd:`Ctrl+Shift+G`
     - Source Control.
   * - :kbd:`Ctrl+Shift+X`
     - Extensions.
   * - :kbd:`Ctrl+Shift+J`
     - Docs.
   * - :kbd:`Ctrl+Shift+U`
     - Output.
   * - :kbd:`Ctrl+\``
     - Terminal.
   * - :kbd:`Ctrl+,`
     - Settings.
   * - :kbd:`Ctrl+.`
     - Quick fixes.
   * - :kbd:`F12`
     - Go to a label.
